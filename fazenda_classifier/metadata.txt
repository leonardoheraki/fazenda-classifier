name=Fazenda Classifier
description=Forest inventory and UAV remote sensing: sampling plots, canopy height, crown measurements and georeferenced CSV export.
about=Experimental public inventory edition derived from RC17. Creates 100 x 100 m sampling plots with 100 height-sampling windows of 1 x 10 m, with interactive placement and rotation. Samples DSM maxima and DTM at the same physical locations. Individual-tree tools measure pointwise DSM-DTM height, manually delineated planar crown area and maximum crown span, retain a multi-tree list, and archive masked native-resolution crown GeoTIFFs. Exports georeferenced plot-height CSVs for external Fazenda ECO workflows and measurement-only tree reports. Tree reports do not identify species or provide ready-to-use species-dependent carbon inputs. Uses QGIS, NumPy and GDAL. This public edition does not include species classification, neural-network architectures, model loading or trained weights. It does not calculate DBH, H5, AGC or climate projections. QGIS 4 compatibility is not declared.
